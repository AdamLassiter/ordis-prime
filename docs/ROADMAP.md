# Roadmap

## 1. Acquisition proof

Status: baseline implemented

Complete source snapshots, canonical promotion, linked item/node pages, local
accounts, inventory counts, goals, explainable node recommendations, and profile
export/import. This milestone proves that source data and personal state remain
separate while still producing useful joined answers.

## 2. Time-aware planning

Status: baseline implemented

Add world-state polling, availability windows, dynamic todos, in-app alerts,
then optional web-push and webhook delivery. Alerts are rules over canonical
entities and player goals, not provider-specific payloads.

Provider-neutral snapshots, current availability reads, automatic polling, the
public timer view, static todos, dynamic watches and in-app alerts are
implemented. Static todos support anchored calendar recurrence without
short-month drift. Optional external delivery channels remain.

## 3. Builds and opinions

Status: in progress

Add partial/full versioned loadouts and immutable share snapshots. Import cited
editorial rankings as independent observations and show disagreement before any
derived consensus.

Build drafts, editor/search workflow, class-checked arcanes, effect-selected
Archon Shard slots, immutable public snapshots and a source-faithful editorial
ranking comparison are implemented. Builds retain configuration labels, current
equipment ranks, Forma counts and capacity-upgrade state. Equipment slots and
specialized weapon arcanes enforce cited canonical classes. Player-authored mod
slot roles and polarities are preserved independently. Calculated capacity,
including Update 38.5 rank progression, upgrades, adjusted drain and special
slot bonuses, is implemented with explicit partial results. Sourced factory
polarity role/count sets are visible without inventing grid positions. Modular
Kitgun and Zaw parts now retain cited canonical roles and compose into builds,
shares, profile transfer and acquisition goals. Exact factory positions,
companion maximum-rank coverage, derived modular statistics, and richer ranking
methodology remain. An
opt-in ordinal median/range summary is implemented with its assumptions visible.

## 4. Discovery and spatial navigation

Status: in progress

Model quests, mechanics, secrets and unlock prerequisites, then render the same
node graph as an interactive star chart. The map is a view over established IDs
and relations, not a second data model.

Composed node metadata, a planet-grouped chart with inventory-aware filtering,
a searchable canonical progression workspace, and cited typed discovery relations are
implemented. Private discovery progress and filtering are also implemented.
Current Wiki abilities are searchable discovery topics linked to their
Warframes or Archwings.
Progression entries with cited relations open a shareable, filter-preserving one-hop
graph. Its deterministic ring layout is presentation state over canonical
entities and typed relations, not a second coordinate model.
Structured Wiki quest records contribute cited descriptions and explicit
sequence links without treating sequence as an access requirement.
Structured mission records classify their explicit bosses and link them to
canonical nodes; same-name WFCD enemies are refined without duplicating their
identity.
An independently scheduled structured enemy adapter classifies every explicit
Wiki `Boss` record without guessing from internal names. A live promotion now
covers every declared partition, including Anarchs, and preserves distinct
source-qualified variants when display names repeat. Mixed region and mission
labels remain deliberately unlinked.
Wiki mission data supplies the attributed current node and connection set, and
those cited star-chart edges compose into node reads symmetrically. A shareable
focus view supports direct traversal and a deterministic one-hop SVG topology
without manufacturing domain coordinates. Stable global or multi-hop
coordinates and richer quest-step progress remain.
Private node completion tracking, chart filtering and portable profile transfer
are implemented independently of sourced access evidence. A filtered-scope bulk
editor supports initial and ongoing completion entry without changing hidden
nodes.
Sourced Mastery Rank gates, including exact Wiki mission requirements, compose
with portable private player progression; the Wiki mission source also
contributes complete allow-listed quest and node-completion requirements.
Railjack Intrinsics gates and their complete supported quest combinations
compose separately with a private highest-category rank.
The three current active-adversary confrontation gates compose with a portable
private none-or-kind setting while retaining their Intrinsics or quest clauses.
Elite Sanctuary Onslaught retains both its quest clause and a cited level-30
Warframe gate, composed with a portable highest-currently-available rank.
The star chart derives and filters the same player-aware access states used by
planning.
Exact Animo Nav Beacon, Judgement Point, Grendel Locator and Mutalist Alad V
Assassinate Key quantity gates now compose with private inventory. Junction
objective descriptions and hints are now imported as ordered, cited public
facts. Portable private objective completion now composes into Junction access
for the star chart and planner. Typed one-time objective and Junction-completion
rewards are linked in both node and item views without entering repeatable drop
recommendations. Their separately labeled inventory overlay also participates
in the chart's unowned-reward filter without conflating grant and drop counts.
Missing planner requirements surface these grants as separately cited one-time
options rather than scored candidate drops.
Other item or equipment conditions remain.
